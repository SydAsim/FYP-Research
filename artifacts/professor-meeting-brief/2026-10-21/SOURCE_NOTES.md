# Source and claim boundaries

Reviewed 21 October 2026. The public-source check is bounded positioning support, not a systematic novelty search.

| Source | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| AWS Builders' Library, Making retries safe with idempotent APIs | Idempotent request identity, retry semantics and changed-intent handling are established engineering concerns. | That AWS or any other provider lacks a complete application-specific speech-permission/repair layer. |
| LiveKit Turns overview | Built-in interruption handling and truncation of conversation history. | Human understanding; transactional rollback; absence of custom application verification in LiveKit deployments. |
| Twilio Media Streams WebSocket messages | Mark/clear protocol and buffered playback control, with marks returned after completion or clear. | That a mark alone proves normal playback or a listener's comprehension. |
| tau-Voice, arXiv:2603.13686 | Existing grounded full-duplex voice benchmark with realistic audio and backend task completion. | Direct comparability to this deterministic typed-intent/tone protocol campaign or an exhaustive novelty comparison. |
| Evaluating Real-Time Voice Agents, arXiv:2609.30798 | Existing discussion of grounded outcomes, timing and recovery. | Peer-reviewed validation of this FYP or priority for the proposed mechanism. |

Official URLs are in MEETING_BRIEF.md. No third-party blog or search-result anecdote was used to establish an industry-wide capability gap. No claim that this is the first project to verify voice-agent outcomes is warranted.
