## Pause and shutdown: existing behaviour

A protocol/resource/source/evidence failure durably stops **new admissions**. An already active collector may continue its original window and evidence work when the reviewed rules permit. A parent pause or process exit does **not** immediately stop AWS compute. The watchdog also does not shut the host down merely because it records an admission pause.

The unchanged watchdog checks on a five-second loop and requests host shutdown when the **original absolute deadline** is reached. The separately installed persistent systemd hard-stop timer invokes `/sbin/shutdown -h now` at the original calendar deadline, with one-second timer accuracy. The calendar representation truncates the fractional second conservatively; the policy deadline remains `2026-10-22T15:24:23.810674Z`. Operating-system/AWS stop completion and delayed billing are not guaranteed to be instantaneous.

Independent preparation/bootstrap timers provide the reviewed bounded prelaunch stop, and are disabled only after verified campaign activation. They are not a new post-pause shutdown feature. Following a campaign pause, the reviewed operator procedure preserves/verifies evidence and explicitly stops the host; absent that action, compute may remain running until the absolute hard stop. The latest false pause was followed by a verified manual stop on 8 October. This amendment changes **no** shutdown policy and adds **no** automatic shutdown.
