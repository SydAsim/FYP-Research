## Did the project produce a research result?

Yes: a completed paired systems/protocol study and preserved evidence, including
a useful negative primary finding. All three implementations have identical
paired business outcomes (455/500 overall; 155/200 correction). The explicit
withdrawal endpoint differs in the 33 designated recovery inputs, although ordinary
supported updates succeed in all three arms. That distinction is worth reporting.

No breakthrough is demonstrated. The positive endpoint recognizes a fixed
correction prefix, uses scripted tone/ACK evidence, and cannot isolate durability
from the rest of the repair/output package. It is not proof of restored human
understanding, general LLM reliability, or superiority to the research literature.
Publication is a possible outcome of suitable venue selection and peer review,
not a property established by completing 1,500 executions or obtaining a small
diagnostic p-value.
